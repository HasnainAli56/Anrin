Cookie Policy

How ANRIN Nordic AB uses essential technical cookies and language preference storage.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember your preferences (such as language selection) and ensure optimal page rendering speed.

2. How We Use Cookies

ANRIN Nordic uses strictly necessary technical cookies and local storage tokens:

  • Language Storage (anrin_lang): Remembers your chosen language (English, Swedish, Finnish, Danish, Norwegian, Icelandic, German) across pages.
  • Translation State (googtrans): Maintains translation state for non-tagged elements.
  • Session Optimization: Ensures fast image caching and smooth navigation across product categories.

3. Managing & Disabling Cookies

You can manage or delete cookies at any time through your web browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may reset your language preference to English upon page reload.
