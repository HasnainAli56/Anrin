Slotted grating OvalGrip Design

PRODUCT DESCRIPTION −
ANRIN's unique grate design creates striking, contrasting accents and fits in well with both modern architectural surroundings and historic settings. With its large inlet cross-section, the textured surface guarantees high water absorption and good grip for both vehicles and pedestrians.
PRODUCT INFORMATION +
Material: Ductile iron (KTL-coated) / Stainless steel / Galvanized steel
Load Classes: C250, D400, E600, F900
Compatibility: Fits ANRIN KE-100, SF-100, Z-100 and SELF-100 channel systems according to EN 1433 standard.
