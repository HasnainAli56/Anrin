Reinforced edge channel KE-200 KTL

  • PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

    The ANRIN KE-200 edge protection channel is a technically advanced drainage system with a 6 mm thick edge protection profile made of galvanized, KTL-coated steel or stainless steel. Suitable for loads from A15 to E600, which allows it to be used in a variety of traffic areas.

  • PRODUCT INFORMATION

    Material polymer concrete
    Length 50 / 100 cm
    Width 26.4 cm
    Height 13. 24. 29. 34 cm cm
    Weight 22.2 - 42.4 kg
    Nominal width 200
    Edge design galvanised steel KTL-coated
    Clasp SnapLock fastening
    Load capacity A15 (Prüfkraft 15 kN) bis E600 (Prüfkraft 600 kN)
