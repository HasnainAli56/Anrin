Sand collecting channel

ANRIN SPORT — Components for edging long jump pits and athletics facilities

SHORT DESCRIPTION & PRODUCT INFORMATION

The ANRIN SPORT sand collecting channel catches sand carried out of long jump pits by athletes during training and competition. It prevents sand from fouling adjacent synthetic running track surfaces, drainage channels, and grass areas, ensuring maximum athlete safety and long-term facility durability according to IAAF regulations.
