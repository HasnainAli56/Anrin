Privacy Policy

How ANRIN Nordic AB collects, protects, and handles personal data under GDPR.

1. Data Protection Commitment

ANRIN Nordic AB is committed to protecting your privacy in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). This policy explains what information we collect when you request a quotation or contact our technical sales team.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only necessary business contact details submitted through our contact forms or quote requests:

  • Full name, business email address, phone number, and company name.
  • Project specifications, required load classes, flow requirements, and delivery address.

3. How Your Data Is Used

Your personal data is used solely to respond to technical inquiries, generate quotations within 24 hours, process logistics from our Trelleborg central warehouse, and deliver customer support throughout your project lifetime.

4. Your Rights & Data Contact

Under GDPR, you have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data at any time. We never sell your personal information to third parties. For data inquiries, please email info@anrin.se.
