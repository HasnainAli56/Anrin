  • PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

    The ANRIN KE-100 edge protection channel is a technically advanced drainage system with a 6 mm thick edge protection profile made of galvanized, KTL-coated steel or stainless steel. Suitable for loads from A15 to E600, which allows it to be used in a variety of traffic areas.
