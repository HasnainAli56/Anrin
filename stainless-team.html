Stainless Team Sweden is a partnership between ANRIN Nordic and Stainless Team Finland OY. We are known for high customer satisfaction, good service, broad expertise and very high quality — in construction, food and shipbuilding industries.

From idea to finished product: with our technical backoffice we create 3D drawings of your products. If you don't find what you're looking for in our range below, we can most likely manufacture it to order.
