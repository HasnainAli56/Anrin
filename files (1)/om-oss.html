About us

Our history

ANRIN Nordic was founded in 2017 as the first wholly-owned subsidiary outside Germany's borders of the parent company ANRIN, which has been manufacturing drainage channels in Anröchte, Germany since the early 1970s.

Since its inception, we have carefully selected and established unique agreements with several manufacturers to offer the Swedish market a complete range in surface water management — linear drainage in polymer concrete, covers in cast iron, stainless steel and separators.

Our head office is in Stockholm and the central warehouse in Trelleborg, where we stock over 100 different products for fast and secure deliveries throughout Sweden and the Nordics. Our sales organisation stretches from Malmö in the south to Östersund in the north.
