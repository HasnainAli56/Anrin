(60 x 40 mm | 70 x 50 mm) SELF Tritt-Schuhabstreifer

  • PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

    SELF boot scrapers are integrated into the floor surface to keep entrance areas in residential buildings clean and dry while also giving them a representative appearance. This ensures that dirt always stays outside the front door, even with heavy use. A modern diamond-shaped grating made of galvanized steel provides a robust and durable cover.

  • PRODUCT INFORMATION

    Material polymer concrete
    Length 60 cm | 75 cm
    Width 40 cm | 50 cm
    Height 8 cm
    Weight 13.3 kg | 21.4 kg
