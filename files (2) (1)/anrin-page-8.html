(75 x 50 mm) SELF comfort boot scrapers

  • PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

    SELF comfort boot scrapers are integrated into the floor surface to keep entrance areas in residential buildings clean and dry while also giving them a representative appearance. This ensures that dirt always stays outside the front door, even with heavy use. Modern diamond or mesh grates made of galvanized steel and aluminum grates with brush, rubber, or needle felt strips offer the right solution for every application.

  • PRODUCT INFORMATION

    Material polymer concrete
    Length 75 cm
    Width 50 cm
    Height 8 cm
    Weight 19.7 kg
    Edge design galvanised steel
