Turf edge plate

  • PRODUCT INFORMATION

    SHORT DESCRIPTION

    The ANRIN SPORT polymer concrete turf edge plate is specially constructed for the external edging of running tracks in arenas. The interlocking groove on one side enables the plastic covering to be processed precisely. For simple and clean installation, the end faces are equipped with a tongue and groove system. The profiled underside of the lawn edge plate and 4 symmetrically arranged stirrup anchors guarantee a permanent and frost-proof connection to the strip foundation.

    Material polymer concrete
    Length 100 cm
    Width 25.0 cm
    Height 6.5 cm
    Weight 19.0 kg
