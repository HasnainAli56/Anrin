Channel 125, type R.1-R.4 (R.1 - R.4)

  • PRODUCT INFORMATION

    SHORT DESCRIPTION

    The NW 125 slotted channel was developed for the increasing number of arenas with level playing fields. This system ensures maximum variability with regard to different areas of the installation. The elimination of tripping hazards increases the comfort of use and simplifies maintenance. In order to comply with the regulations of the International Association of Athletics Federations, it is sufficient, where necessary (e.g. in case of a competition) to provide the channel bodies along the inside of the running track with a mobile frame or plastic cover.

    Material polymer concrete
    Length 100 cm
    Width 16.0 cm
    Height 18.7 - 20.0 cm
    Weight 24.0 - 29.0 kg
